## Step 1: First nudge (day 0)
Subject: How did we do on {{project_or_ref}}?
Body:
Hi {{recipient_first_name}},

Thank you for choosing us for {{project_or_ref}}. If you have two minutes, a short review would help us a lot: {{review_link}}

If anything wasn't right, reply to me directly and I'll fix it first.

Thanks,
{{sender_name}}, {{sender_company}}

## Step 2: Second reminder (day 5)
Subject: A quick favour about {{project_or_ref}}
Body:
Hi {{recipient_first_name}},

A gentle reminder in case my last note got buried. An honest review, even a sentence or two, helps other customers and helps us improve: {{review_link}}

Thank you,
{{sender_name}}, {{sender_company}}

## Step 3: Final notice (day 12)
Subject: Last note about your review
Body:
Hi {{recipient_first_name}},

This is my last note about this. If you'd like to share your experience of {{project_or_ref}}, the link is here: {{review_link}} No problem at all if not, and thank you again for your business.

Best wishes,
{{sender_name}}, {{sender_company}}
